A Note for Mummy and Daddy

Sharing is the very first step towards division, which your child will meet in Grade 2 and Grade 3. At this stage your child does not need to calculate anything. They need to give out one at a time, count while they do it, and see that the groups are the same.

Please share things at home every day:

  • Share the grapes between the children.
  • Share the chips onto two plates.
  • Share the pegs, the blocks, the crayons, the sweets.

Let your child hand them out one at a time, counting out loud while they do it. Counting is the foundation of everything in Mathematics, so every count is time well spent.

Then ask: “Did everybody get the same?”
